Welcome to

Montessori School, Vidyanagar

Montessori School, Vidyanagar is a co-educational day school, established under the aegis of Montessori School Committee, as a valued branch of the Montessori family of schools. Located in the Vidyanagar area of Kurnool city, the school offers a warm, well-appointed campus with state-of-the-art facilities in a specifically designed ambience, replete with centralized AC classrooms and dedicated sporting facilities.

Through modern learning-teaching techniques, the school endeavours to make education a happy, value-based and progressive journey that creates the global citizens of tomorrow.

About Us

Explore, Experience & Excel at Montessori

Through the pursuance of excellence in education, Montessori — with the traditional values of empathy, respect and discipline — renders a liberal education where the mind flowers without fear. A vision that accentuates human excellence and moulds the younger generation for a meaningful and productive life in a globalized society.

Our Vision

To create citizens of tomorrow steeped in the traditional values of empathy, discipline and respect — where no fears prevail; a vision that leads to change.
